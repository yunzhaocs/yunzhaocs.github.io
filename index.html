About

Dr. Yun Zhao is a research scientist at Meta Platforms, Inc. at Menlo Park, California, United States. He received his PhD degree in Computer Science from the University of California, Santa Barbara in April 2021 (advisor: Prof. Linda Petzold). Before that, he received his M.E. from Tsinghua University (advisor: Prof. Zhisheng Niu) and B.E. from Zhejiang University. He is the recipient of the Best Paper Award (DMBIH’21) and the Best Student Paper Award (ACM-BCB’22).
